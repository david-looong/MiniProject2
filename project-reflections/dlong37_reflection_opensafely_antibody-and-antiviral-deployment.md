# Reflection: opensafely_antibody-and-antiviral-deployment

This project's overall pattern is declining, trailing toward little activity over its
recorded history, with two gaps of three or more months, the fewest of the projects that 
have any gap at all.

The longest gap (2023-02 to 2024-01, 12 months) was moderately easy to interpret. The
pre-gap commits (report-writing activity, plus several same-day reverted changes to
`data_preparation.do`) read like a study being wrapped up rather than a mid-project stall,
and the README confirms this repo produced a specific preprint on antivirals and
neutralising-antibody deployment trends. Most likely, once that report was finished, the 
original scope was satisfied.

Recovery came under a different lead: Rose Higgins picks up the post-gap commits in
February 2024, rather than the earlier contributors (Linda Nab, bangzheng2217). Notably, 
the later post-gap commits add "long COVID codelists", which is evidence the project was 
reactivated tobextend the original study to a new research question, not to keep fixing the old one.

**Current status:** Inactive (last GitHub commit 2024-02-13).
