# Reflection: darioizzo_geodesynets

geodesyNets shows a steady overall pattern punctuated by the second-longest gap of any of
the ten projects, with four gaps of three or more months across its history.

The longest gap (2023-03 to 2024-11, 21 months) was fairly easy to interpret once the
README was checked. The pre-gap commits end with messages like "cleanup and synch after
rebuttal and substantial additional work," which directly ties the work to a paper's peer
review cycle. This is research code (Izzo & Gomez's geodesyNets paper), and once that
publication cycle wrapped up there was no dedicated maintainer time until something else
brought people back.

Recovery came from a new contributor, Pietro Fanti, who began pushing feature and fix
commits (a quadratic-layer implementation, a CLI training script, notebook updates) in
December 2024, which was merged in by original author Dario Izzo. This observation is consistent with a new student or collaborator picking the codebase back up.

**Current status:** Inactive (last GitHub commit 2025-09-16, more than 6 months before the
assignment's cutoff).
